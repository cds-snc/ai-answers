# How to create a CanadaLogin

## On this page

- Before you start
- Steps to create a CanadaLogin

## Before you start

You will need the following items to create a CanadaLogin:

- An active email address that you have access to
- Either:
    - An active phone number that you can receive texts or calls to on an ongoing basis
    - OR a device that supports passkey, that you can access on an ongoing basis

We will also ask you for:

- A password
- Your preferred name
- Your preferred official language

## Steps to create a CanadaLogin

When you are ready to create your CanadaLogin, you’ll need to complete the following steps:

### 1. Navigate to a service that’s using CanadaLogin

If you are already on a service that’s asking you to use CanadaLogin, you can skip this step.

Otherwise, to create a CanadaLogin:

1. Go to one of our participating services and select “Sign in with CanadaLogin”. You will be redirected to the sign in page.
2. Select “Create a CanadaLogin”.
3. Then, follow the steps below to create a CanadaLogin.

### 2. Read through the privacy notice

- This contains important information about how we use and store your personal information, and who we share it with.
- Once you have read through the information, select “Continue”.

### 3. Read through and agree to the Terms and Conditions

- This contains important information about how to keep your CanadaLogin secure, and how we keep it secure.
- Once you have read through the information, agree to the Terms by checking the checkbox, and then select “Continue”.

### 4. Enter your name

Enter your name. If you only have one name, enter it into the last name field.

> **Related help guides**
>
> - [Problems with creating a profile](https://login.canada.ca/en/users/trouble-signing-up/issues-creating-profile/)

### 5. Enter your email address and choose your language preference

We recommend you choose an email address that you’ll always be able to access.

If you are accessing a service for personal use, we recommend using a personal email address that you have access to.

If you are accessing a service on behalf of a business, we recommend using a business email that you have access to.

It is acceptable to have more than one CanadaLogin (e.g. a personal and a business CanadaLogin).

### 6. Check your email for a message from “CanadaLogin”

- We’ll send a 6-digit one-time code to your email. It may take a few minutes to arrive. Return to the CanadaLogin webpage and enter the one-time code.
- Emails can only be used for one CanadaLogin.
    - If you attempt to use an email address that’s already registered to a CanadaLogin, you will receive an error when you enter the code.
    - If you are locked out of your CanadaLogin, you may need to recover your CanadaLogin. If you cannot recover your CanadaLogin, you will need to create another CanadaLogin using a new email address.

> **Related help guides**
>
> - [Problems entering and verifying your email](https://login.canada.ca/en/users/trouble-signing-up/issues-enter-verify-email/)

### 7. Create your password

Passwords must be **at least 12 characters** and can be **up to 65 characters** long. They should not be a commonly used password or phrase (e.g. “let me in” or “password”).

#### How to make a strong password

To help prevent your CanadaLogin and your personal information from being compromised, consider strengthening your password by using:

- Unique phrases that only you recognize
- A passphrase instead of a single word. For example, combine multiple words with spaces (e.g., “pillow moose dish”)
- Numbers and special characters
- A longer password that you can still remember

Avoid easy-to-guess passwords like ones that use:

- Common phrases or repeated characters, like abc or 111
- Parts of your email address or personal dates, like your birthday
- Numbers and special characters
- The same or similar password that you use for other accounts, such as your bank account or email

> **Related help guides**
>
> - [Problems creating a password](https://login.canada.ca/en/users/trouble-signing-up/issues-creating-password/)

### 8. Set up a 2-step verification method

As an added layer of protection, CanadaLogin requires you to set up a second authentication method to keep your CanadaLogin secure.

This is referred to as 2-step verification. If you want to know more about 2-step verification, see our [information guide on 2-step verification](https://login.canada.ca/en/users/get-started/two-step-verification-methods/).

To set up 2-step verification, follow these steps:

1. Choose your 2-step verification method and select “Continue”.
2. Follow the steps for whichever method you chose:
    - Option 1: Text (SMS) or Voice call
        - Enter your phone number and select “Continue”.
        - Verify your number. You should have received either a text message (SMS) or phone call with a 6-digit verification code. Enter the 6-digit code into the site when prompted.
    - Option 2: Passkey or security key
        - Select “Continue”.
        - Select “Create a passkey”.
        - Follow the prompts on your screen for your device or password manager.
        - Name your passkey and select “Continue”.

> **Related help guides**

### 9. Add a recovery method (optional)

Setting up a recovery method helps make sure you don’t get locked out. We highly recommend adding a recovery method.

To add a recovery method, follow these steps:

1. Choose your 2-step verification method and select “Continue”.
2. Follow the steps for whichever method you chose:
    - Option 1: Text (SMS) or Voice call
        - Enter your phone number and select “Continue”.
        - Verify your number. You should have received either a text message (SMS) or phone call with a 6-digit verification code. Enter the 6-digit code into the site when prompted.
    - Option 2: Passkey or security key
        - Select “Continue”.
        - Select “Create a passkey”.
        - Follow the prompts on your screen for your device or password manager.
        - Name your passkey and select “Continue”.

### 10. You’ve created your CanadaLogin

- You will see what data CanadaLogin will share to the service you’re trying to access. Continue to access the service.
- You will be able to connect your CanadaLogin to other participating programs and services, when they become available.
- You can add additional 2-step verification methods, or update your information by selecting the “Manage CanadaLogin” link in the menu bar once you’re signed in.
    - You can find this link in the menu of any service you have connected to your CanadaLogin.

# Updating your sign-in method to CanadaLogin

CanadaLogin is replacing the following sign-in methods:

- GCKey username and password

CanadaLogin is temporarily replacing the following sign-in methods:

- Interac sign-in partners (banks and credit unions)

> **Related help guides**
>
> - [Why CanadaLogin is replacing GCKey and Interac sign-in partners](https://login.canada.ca/en/users/get-started/replacing-existing-signin-methods/)

Some services will start using CanadaLogin before others. Until every service uses CanadaLogin, you may need to continue using other sign-in methods.  

**You’ll need to update your sign-in method on each service where you already have an account.** You should receive an email when services you use start using CanadaLogin.

## How to update your sign-in method to CanadaLogin

- Sign in to a migrating service.
- Select the sign-in method you used the last time you signed in to the service. You’ll see instructions to update your sign-in method. Follow the steps
    1. Create a CanadaLogin or use your existing CanadaLogin.
    2. Sign in with GCKey or Interac sign-in. Select the method you used the last time you signed in to this account.
        - If there was only 1 old sign-in method, you may not have to choose.
        - You may have to complete GCKey’s or the Interac sign-in partner’s multi-factor authentication
    3. You can now use CanadaLogin to access your account. You can use the same CanadaLogin to accessing other participating services.

### If your account is not found

After you update your sign-in method, you may not have access to your existing account on the service. This could be one of the follow reasons:

- You used a different GCKey or Interac sign-in the last time you accessed the service.
- You have never signed in to this service before.

If you have previously signed in to this service, but used the wrong GCKey or Interac sign-in partner, you’ll need to create a new CanadaLogin. Use a different email address, and go through the linking process again using the correct GCKey or Interac sign-in partner.

> **Related help guides**
>
> - [Managing multiple CanadaLogins from the same email inbox](https://login.canada.ca/en/users/get-started/managing-multiple-canadalogins/)

It’s possible that you may not have an account on this service. If that is the case, you’ll need to create a new account on the service.

### If your old sign-in method has expired

If you have not signed in with your Interac sign-in partner in over a year, your sign-in method may have expired.

If you have not signed in with GCKey in over 2 years, your sign-in method may have expired.

You’ll need to create a new account for the program or service you are trying to access. You may be able to recover your account on the program or service.

You may be able to recover your account on the service. Each service’s account recovery process is different. Please refer to the specific service’s help content or contact their call centre for help.

## Counter tariffs — which products are covered

* Counter tariffs: ALWAYS ⚠️DOWNLOAD this authoritative source for the full list of products subject to counter tariffs https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/programs/international-trade-finance-policy/canadas-response-us-tariffs/complete-list-us-products-subject-to-counter-tariffs.html https://www.canada.ca/fr/ministere-finances/programmes/politiques-finances-echanges-internationaux/reponse-canada-droits-douane-americains/liste-complete-produits-americains-assujettis-contre-mesures-tarifaires.html
    - Lists stacked newest-first under effective-date headings ("Effective…"/"En vigueur…"). Only the top list (8-Sept-2026) is in force — it already includes carried-over tariffs like autos and steel. Lists below it ended; historical questions only.
    - Filter above table requires user to enter exact text - eg. "motor vehicles" not "automobiles"
    - Tariff items (HS codes), not products; no official sanction, read with the Schedule to Canada's Customs Tariff. Quote the item and rate you found and link the page. Not finding a product is not proof it's exempt.
        - AVOID out of date: https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2025/03/list-of-products-from-the-united-states-subject-to-25-per-cent-tariffs-effective-march-13-2025.html https://www.canada.ca/fr/ministere-finances/nouvelles/2025/03/liste-des-produits-en-provenance-des-etats-unis-assujettis-a-des-droits-de-douane-de-25--a-compter-du-13-mars-2025.html
* Counter tariffs only apply to goods originating from the U.S., in accordance with the Determination of Country of Origin for the Purpose of Marking Goods (CUSMA Countries) Regulations http://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-94-23 https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/fra/reglements/DORS-94-23/TexteComplet.html
